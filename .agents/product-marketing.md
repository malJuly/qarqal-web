# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v3
**Last updated:** 2026-10-20

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Car and motorbike insurance, road tax and 24/7 towing in one Malaysian app.
**What it does:** QarQal lets Malaysian vehicle owners compare motor insurance quotes from up to 10 insurers, add extra coverage and pay in the app. The same app renews road tax, offers Qarqal Assist towing and breakdown packages, and keeps every vehicle in Qarqal Garage.
**Product category:** Motor insurance and vehicle services app (consumer, Malaysia)
**Product type:** Mobile app (App Store, Google Play, Huawei AppGallery)
**Business model:** Commission on insurance; paid Qarqal Assist packages (Basic, Premium)

## Target Audience
**Primary:** Malaysian car and motorbike owners who renew insurance and road tax every year. Also covers company-registered vehicles.
**Primary use case:** Get and pay for a motor insurance renewal without going through an agent or several websites.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Renew insurance and road tax on time with little effort
- Know the price is fair by seeing several insurers side by side
- Have someone to call when the car breaks down
**Use cases:**
- Insurance due next month, wants quotes tonight from the phone
- Breakdown on the highway, needs towing (including EVs and superbikes)
- Owns more than one vehicle and keeps losing track of renewal dates

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Renewal is scattered. Insurance through an agent on WhatsApp, prices checked one insurer site at a time, road tax, towing and summons all in different places.
**Why the old way falls short:**
- Waiting on an agent's reply to get one quote
- No easy side-by-side comparison
- Separate portals and apps for each task
**Emotional tension:** Fear of overpaying, forgetting the renewal date, being stranded with no one to call.

## Competitive Landscape
**Rule:** Never name competitors. Compare against "the old way" (agent, multiple sites, multiple portals).

## Differentiation
- Up to 10 insurers compared in one place
- Insurance, road tax, towing and vehicle records in one app
- Authorised JPJ Rakan Niaga (JPJ services integration coming soon)
- 24/7 towing and breakdown, including EVs and superbikes

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Is this legit / safe to pay here? | Registered Malaysian company (July Systems Tech Sdn. Bhd.), authorised JPJ Rakan Niaga, policies issued by the insurers themselves |
| Is it more expensive than my agent? | Compare quotes from up to 10 insurers side by side before paying |
| Another app to install? | Replaces several apps and portals |

## Customer Language
**Words to use:** renew, road tax, insurance, towing, quote, compare, cover
**Words to avoid:** seamless, revolutionary, unlock, ecosystem, solution, one-stop (overused)
**Glossary:** Qarqal Garage (saved vehicles), Qarqal Assist (towing/breakdown packages), Rakan Niaga (JPJ authorised business partner)

## Brand Voice
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean |
